A bridge into silicon photonics.

For an optics PhD working at Intel in Santa Clara, the strongest initial directions are PIC device design, photonic characterization/test, and integration. Semiconductor process literacy, RF interfaces, PDK layout, statistical analysis and packaging are the practical bridge from existing optics expertise.

Prioritize Santa Clara teams at Xscape, OpenLight and Marvell/Celestial AI; also investigate Ayar Labs in north San Jose and Lightmatter in Mountain View. ANELLO adds an integrated sensing route, and PsiQuantum adds a quantum photonics route. Intel’s optical I/O work makes an internal move worth investigating. This prioritization is an inference from locations and technical focus, not a guarantee of openings or hiring eligibility.

Courses and classes

Begin with a hands-on design-and-data course rather than repeating introductory optics. Select focused AIM modules for specific gaps. University syllabi are curriculum references and do not imply public enrollment. Verify fees, dates, tool licenses and fabrication access before committing.

  • UBCx · Silicon Photonics Design, Fabrication and Data Analysis
    Hands-on modeling, mask layout, fabrication and measured data analysis. A strong bridge from optics into PIC work. Instructor-paced; check current enrollment and fabrication arrangements.
  • AIM Photonics · Online courses
    Focused modules in fundamentals, passive test, active test, detectors and modulators, simulation, and sensing. Check registration, pricing and prerequisites on the provider site.
  • MIT Microphotonics · Teaching packages
    Integrated silicon photonics and photonic materials/device teaching packages. Useful structured background alongside device exercises.
  • Southampton · Silicon Photonics OPTO6007
    A university module covering guided waves, passive and active devices, fabrication and integration. Use as a syllabus reference; public listing does not imply open remote enrollment.
  • ePIXfab · Training catalog
    Specialist silicon photonics training, schools and workshops. Mostly event-based; location, availability and dates vary.
  • Ansys Optics · Learning and device examples
    Official solver tutorials and device workflows. Commercial software access may be needed to run examples.

A suggested twelve-week transition

  1. Weeks 1–2: Review SOI/SiN, guided modes and passive circuits. Model one waveguide across wavelength and geometry.
  2. Weeks 3–4: Design a testable MZI or ring; add references and build a PDK-compliant layout.
  3. Weeks 5–6: Study semiconductor modulators, germanium detectors and electrical interfaces; calculate a link budget.
  4. Weeks 7–8: Analyze measured course/public/authorized data; extract loss, group index and Q with uncertainty.
  5. Weeks 9–10: Budget optical coupling, source power, thermal drift and assembly yield.
  6. Weeks 11–12: Package one complete reproducible study for a selected role, explaining assumptions, results and limitations.
